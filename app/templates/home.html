How the flow works

  1. Admin registers taxis and rank agents (Admin page).
  2. Driver joins the feeder park queue from the Driver App and sees their position and estimated wait.
  3. A rank agent calls the next taxi forward for their terminal; the driver gets a notification telling them which terminal to go to.
  4. The passenger tells the rank agent their destination; the agent logs it, and the system classifies it as Standard, Local, or Fares Fare.
  5. The driver marks the fare complete. Standard fares rejoin the back of the feeder park automatically. Local / Fares Fare exemptions instead get a return deadline and can check back in directly at a terminal of their choice.
  6. The Feeder Park Screen shows badge numbers of drivers due to be called within the next few minutes.
